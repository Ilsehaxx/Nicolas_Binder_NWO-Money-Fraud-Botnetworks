# Nicolas Binder Biography

## Overview
Nicolas Binder is a German electronic music producer and DJ. He is known for producing and DJing in the electronic music scene, particularly in genres like house, techno, and rave music.

## Career Start
- Started his career in electronic music production.
- Debut EP: "Runner" released in May 2020.

## Breakthrough
- Achieved massive success with the song "Moment" (originally 2021).
- "Moment" has over 60 million streams on Spotify.
- Reached position 90 in the German single charts.
- Included in multiple Spotify playlists.
- Sample from Neelix's "The Twenty Five" and dialogue from the film "Absolute Giganten" (1998).
- Re-released in 2023 via Epic Records with a short version.
- "Moment" went viral, positioned between rave and trance genres (from POP-HIMMEL).

## Other Hits
